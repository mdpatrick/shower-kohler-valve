What stays the same

8 mm face · 2 mm collar wall · 24 mm maximum collar depth with reliefs above the first 12 mm · 3 mm tabs · 12 × 8 mm slots. The slots allow adjustment; independent padded bracing supports the form.

Tell your friend

PETG, millimeters, 100% scale. Import one STL or geometry-only 3MF and use the printer’s profile. Start with 0.2 mm layers, 5 walls and 25% gyroid/cubic infill; follow the guide for orientation and slicing checks. Do not shrink the model to fit a bed.
