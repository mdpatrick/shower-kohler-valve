THE PROJECT / FOR YOUR FRIEND

Why we’re printing this.

Go to the two selected print files →

The travertine opening around the shower controls was over-cut. The printed form helps guide a supported repair around that opening while keeping the valve accessible. After the form is removed, the custom escutcheon plates sit behind the Kohler trim.

Your print’s job: the outer face guides the repair surface, the collar preserves the central opening, and the slotted tabs help locate the tool on the rods. The installer braces it, rehearses fit and removal, and removes it after the repair. Final trim fit and sealing against shower spray are separate installation steps.

Both primary forms have the same deep collar

24 mm maximum collar depth, with an uninterrupted front 12 mm band below the rear clearance reliefs. That band is part of each deep form; it is not the separate short model. Both retain the 8 mm face, 2 mm collar wall, 3 mm tabs and 12 × 8 mm slots. Use independent padded bracing.

Tell your friend

PETG, millimeters, 100% scale. Import one STL or geometry-only 3MF and use the printer’s profile. Start with 0.2 mm layers, 5 walls and 25% gyroid/cubic infill; follow the guide for orientation and slicing checks. Do not shrink the model to fit a bed.
