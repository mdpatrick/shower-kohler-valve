What the supplied measurements tell us

Mounting pattern needs reconciliation

With equal 5.66 mm small holes, the measured gap implies 112.05 mm centers. Captured tip centers give 108.01 mm. Best rigid alignment still misses by 2.02 mm per hole; the assumed stud allows only 0.33 mm radial clearance.

That flags the current reconstruction. It does not prove the actual plate is wrong: tip picking, stud angle, NE hole diameter and scale remain uncertain.

The studs remain too far forward in the model

The thin backing plate shifts the trial surface by 2.524 mm including a 1 mm illustrative seal gap. The unchanged stud tips still exceed the assumed cover rear by about 9.59 and 13.65 mm. The thicker option also fails this comparison.

No cut length or drilling instruction follows from those estimates. Identify the same fasteners and verify factory adjustment, nut seats, cover cavity and retained threads.

The cover's control spacing is plausible

The 46.25 mm inside-edge gap and 35.23 mm lower-edge-to-rim gap are now confirmed. Provisionally using a 33.79 mm lower bore still gives 80.04 mm centers. Only that lower bore remains unmeasured in this spacing calculation; no F005 outline or aperture changes result.

Actual plates and reconstructed references

The independent inside-solid calculation finds 149-164 captured points inside the provisional inner shell across trial foam depths, and 202 inside the cover shell. All detected points are in stud neighborhoods; none are in control neighborhoods at these trial poses. This flags unresolved model fit, not a proven defect in the actual plates. The rear foam trace is the deciding aperture check

The reported 6 mm band is preserved. Its actual rear location is unknown. The zero-inset scenario leaves about 1.45 mm from the aperture to the foam; a 1.5 mm inward shift produces a small intersection. Trace both actual foam edges on the supplied full-size template.

Coverage and captured-surface checks

51,929 full-resolution triangles were clipped to the forward installation path and the eroded main opening. Zero lie outside the shaped aperture. Minimum sampled clearance is 1.55 mm. Hidden geometry, the excluded 3 mm stone boundary strip and scan-scale uncertainty remain. The 80 mm diverter aperture and its factory rear assembly require a separate check.

Assembly and corrections

  1. Sound wall/support and a qualified wall-to-new-plate surface seal.
  2. Custom backing plate, selected thickness.
  3. Original foam on a continuous supported landing.
  4. Retained factory inner plate, lying flat.
  5. Factory nuts behind the decorative cover, with studs correctly adjusted (obscured in this view).
  6. Cover notch down; correct adapters, bonnets and handles; full operation and drainage.

The diverter retains its own factory mounting plate/foam. Its hidden assembly has not been dimensionally reconstructed. No additional holes in the travertine or the decorative covers are proposed.

Shower-water resistance depends on these interfaces together. Covering the cutout alone does not prove a watertight assembly or restore an unseen membrane.
