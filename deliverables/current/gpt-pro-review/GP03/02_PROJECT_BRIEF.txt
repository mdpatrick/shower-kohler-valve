4. INSTALLED TIP DISTANCES AND WHY THEY DO NOT LOCATE HOLES

U = upper control tip; L = lower control tip; NE = upper-right trim stud tip; SW = lower-left stud tip, viewed from the shower.

All six readings are straight-line 3D distances between the centers of the end faces of the projecting pieces, at unequal Z depths:
U-L 80.00; NE-SW 109.80; U-NE 51.40; L-NE 80.25; U-SW 81.14; L-SW 54.70 mm.

These are not wall-plane center distances or axes at the intended backing plane. If a depth difference dz is independently known, projected tip separation is sqrt(distance_3d^2 - dz^2), but even projected tips do not establish inclined stud axes at another plane. No repeat readings/uncertainty bounds were supplied. Earlier technique/endpoint concerns belong to these installed tip readings and must not be generalized to the separately confirmed coplanar inner-plate face measurements. The calipers guide reconciliation, with the primary scan as weaker corroboration. Preserve both; fit weights/residuals are not measurement accuracy.

The conditional loose-plate center spacing 112.05 mm exceeds the 109.8 mm installed 3D tip spacing. Unequal depth alone cannot make a projected tip spacing larger than its 3D length. This deserves reconciliation of contacts, hole diameters, axes and assembly pose; it is not by itself proof of defective hardware.

5. MANUFACTURER REFERENCES AND ROD DEPTH

Primary archived PDFs and source register are included. Applicability to the actual revision must be confirmed. The older 1041504-2-F guide places nuts behind the decorative cover, calls for the inner plate to lie flat and its foam to cover the opening, shows the drain down, and provides adapter/engagement checks. Read the complete applicable steps, not isolated numbers.

Official service part 1038645 is described as Stud, 10-24 x 4.56 L: nominal total length 4.56 inches = 115.824 mm. This is different from permitted exposed front projection. The older trim guide's front-projection maximum is 7/16 inch = 11.1125 mm from the finished wall. Do not assume a custom backing moves that datum or establishes an extension allowance.

The user now confirms rear wood contact. Earlier primary-scan front tip estimates around 26.61 and 30.68 mm suggested roughly 15.5 and 19.6 mm inward travel to the nominal 11.11 mm datum. Those are conditional scan estimates, not hole drilling depths or an adjustment instruction. Required retained threads and actual front/reference planes remain physical checks.

Handle attachment is a separate system from stud/nut adjustment. More clearance behind rods does not repair a wrong control rough-in depth or wrong adapter. The main handles install from the front; rear connection envelopes must reach the stems through the relevant layers. There is no established general 5 mm waterproofing tolerance.

Diverter: outside diameter 124, inside diameter 80 mm. Fallback: 120/80 mm.

Existing exports: six part/stock combinations, each STEP, STL and DXF. No fixed locator tabs or holes. The broad opening contains the modeled control openings and rods.

The no-tab rationale is to preserve adjustment and insertion access under uncertain pose. Proposed trial centering uses the actual covers, four equal visible borders and temporary holding, then verification of retention with the selected support/clamping system. This is not a proven retention design. The expert must establish whether the backing remains stable without excessive nut load.

Visible main border: (220 - 184.15)/2 = 17.925 mm. Diverter border using nominal cover 98.425 mm: (124 - 98.425)/2 = 12.7875 mm. The diverter cover's radial overlap beyond the 80 mm aperture is 9.2125 mm, not proof that its actual rear seal footprint lands there.

Predicted gap between the 220/124 pair is 3.06-5.21 mm across three historical placements. The 120 fallback adds 2 mm gap. Those placements are scenarios, not uncertainty bounds; true cover centers must govern. A 3 mm actual gap is a project handling preference, not a Kohler standard.

Main projected damage margins after prior trace allowances are about 7.03-8.28 mm. Diverter 124 original-scenario margin is about 10.95 mm. Concealed damage, weak stone and unsupported depth are not resolved by those 2D margins.

Requested shop targets, not vendor commitments: metal cut profile +/-0.13 mm; plastic +/-0.25 mm; free overall flatness 0.5 mm. Report actual stock thickness and finish loss. These are not accepted field-fit allowances.

8. DIGITAL CHECKS AND THEIR LIMITS

The current audit records valid single STEP solids; clean two-contour mm DXFs; analytic volume, bounds and watertight STL checks; six consistent part sets; printable two-axis 100 mm bars; drawings and archive hashes; Blender save/reopen. The fresh calculation was by the same local agent, not a separate reviewer.

The capsule covers 51,929 clipped primary-scan projected surface pieces in each of three historical pose hypotheses. Minimum projected hardware clearances are 1.904, 2.303 and 1.606 mm, with no modeled conflicts. Scope is Z >= 0.5 mm and inside OPEN-MAIN eroded 3 mm. Stone-border geometry, hidden surfaces, uncertain axes and the newly attached scan's full insertion path are outside that result. No physical clearance allowance has been demonstrated.

With an assumed 6 mm edge-flush foam band, the nominal extra land between foam inner edge and aperture is only 1 mm. Outer corner-radius scenarios 40-65.175 mm were checked. After a 0.13 mm cut target only 0.87 mm nominal extra land remains, before unresolved footprint and registration error. This is a key design weakness to assess, not an endorsed tolerance stack.

Historical LOC01 used added tabs, exploratory 7.5 mm holes, a 12 x 7.5 mm SW slot, assumed normal stud axes and an earlier diagonal aperture. It reported insertion and seated model conflicts. It is not a released alternative, and its coordinates must not be copied into new cut files. A notch removes material and must be judged separately.

9. MATERIALS, COST AND HANDOFF STATUS

Flat laser-cut stainless is the existing metal route; no metal additive manufacturing is needed for these flat profiles. Earlier research compared 316 deburred/directionally grained with 304 #4 protected brushed stock. Deburring is not necessarily cosmetic finishing, and brushed stainless is not an exact Kohler BN finish match. Thin metal still requires suitable support under seal/clamp loads.

Earlier planning allowances per pair, NOT quotes: FDM plastic $40-120 plus shipping; acrylic $40-100; SendCutSend cut/deburr metal $65-180 before separately priced cosmetic work; OSH Cut finished-route allowance $75-220. Public processing claims included roughly 2-4 business days for standard metal, eligible faster service, and 3-5 business days for one print service. Eligibility, finish time, shipping, tax, destination and actual part pricing were not established. Recheck before buying; no current vendor quote is attached.

Existing seven-page FT01 guide, two-page shop sheets and full-size templates are included as baseline artifacts. They are not a finalized repair specification. The new Pro review should instruct the agent to produce audience-specific evergreen documents and fill only justified repair details.
