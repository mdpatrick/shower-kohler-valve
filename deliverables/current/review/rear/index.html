What the wood contact means

Kohler's trim studs thread into the valve. They do not obtain their trim retention by fastening into wood. You now confirm their rear ends meet the wood, which can prevent further inward adjustment. Kohler installation guide, page 7.

Wood clearance holes are your authorized preferred approach if retraction is needed and other valve/handle depth checks pass. Required retraction, remaining thread engagement and the wood member's role determine the eventual relief dimensions. The nearby pipe and cable routes also need to be accounted for.

Moving a trim stud inward would not by itself change valve-body or handle-stem depth. The U-handle engagement and final gasket/cover seating checks remain separate.
