Surface contact is the leading interpretation of the close-up

The visible rod appears to terminate at the wood surface. A shallow indentation or obscured hole cannot be excluded, and the second stud's rear contact is not visible. The stock studs are machine-threaded trim hardware, not specified wood anchors.

To settle it without guessing, trace each front stud to its rear end and inspect the contact with a mirror or borescope at a grazing angle. Look for the end face sitting on wood versus the shaft disappearing into a hole. A paper feeler passing underneath proves a gap; failure to pass does not distinguish contact from penetration.

A dimensional cross-check is also possible once the actual rod length L is known: measure D, from its front tip to the near wood face along that same rod. If D is approximately L, it touches; if D is less than L, the implied penetration is L − D; if D is greater, the gap is D − L. The difference must exceed measurement uncertainty, and a cut or substituted rod invalidates using 115.8 mm for L.

What the wood contact means

Kohler's trim studs thread into the valve. They do not obtain their trim retention by fastening into wood. If their rear ends already bear on the wood, that contact could prevent sufficient inward adjustment. Kohler installation guide, page 7.

A clearance pocket is an option to evaluate after tracing the same two studs and determining the required retraction, remaining thread engagement and the wood member's role. The photos do not establish a pocket size or authorize choosing drilling over a suitable hardware correction. The nearby pipe and cable routes also need to be accounted for.

Moving a trim stud inward would not by itself change valve-body or handle-stem depth. The U-handle engagement and final gasket/cover seating checks remain separate.
