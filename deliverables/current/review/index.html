Draft repair sequence

This is a review sequence with hold points. It becomes an installation manual only after the repair, material interfaces, measured fit and cure requirements are resolved. No further travertine cutting, grinding, drilling or enlargement is authorized.

01

Record the current installation

Who: Homeowner + installer
Before: Existing access is safe; no pressure parts disturbed. User reported no changes since capture on 2026-10-14.
Tools: Camera, ruler, labels; measurement worksheet.

Take the requested dimensional readings and label the matching locations. Report any future plumbing, stud or wall change before using the model for fit.

Check: Readings reference the worksheet revision; any newly changed areas are identified.

Stop: If a valve moves or repairs hide a cut, the old scan cannot certify current fit.

02

Check plumbing and factory adjustment

Who: Qualified plumber
Before: Exact Kohler models recorded; applicable revision checked.
Tools: Applicable Kohler guides, straightedge, depth gauge and correct hand tools.

Trace reported rear stud contact with wood; verify valve rough-in depth and identify the stem adapters separately. Check available adjustment, final seating and supply/valve leakage before selecting a repair.

Check: Stud rear travel and engagement, Check 10 tip identity/depths, plaster-guard or axis-datum check, and correct adapter/handle seating recorded.

Stop: Do not shorten studs, force trim, or loosen pressure-containing parts for a homeowner measurement. No valve relocation target is approved.

03

Resolve the support and water barrier

Who: Qualified tile/waterproofing installer
Before: Safe access through the existing opening; stone protected.
Tools: Light, inspection mirror, straightedge; approved inspection method.

Identify sound substrate, possible backing access and the existing waterproofing system. Design supported local rebuilding or a retained plate detail from this evidence.

Check: Complete gasket landing, backing/load path and compatible water-barrier connection are documented.

Stop: No filler bridging the unsupported opening. Surface sealant or grout cannot establish an unseen membrane connection. No tile cutting is authorized.

04

Dry fit the reviewed revision

Who: Installer with homeowner review
Before: Measurements accepted; repair option and trial geometry revised.
Tools: Calibrated 1:1 paper template, thickness-accurate trial part or spacers, straightedge, original hardware.

Check the planar template first, then trial the actual layer thickness with the original inner plate, gasket, trim and handles.

Check: Worst cuts concealed with sound overlap; no rocking; continuous foam support; trim seats; threads engage; drain down; full travel and tool/removal access.

Stop: Paper proves no depth clearance. Prototype materials are not approved permanent wet-area materials. Record failures; do not force the fit.

05

Install only the released material detail

Who: Installer / fabricator as assigned
Before: Selected design, material data, physical fit and explicit fabrication approval complete.
Tools: Approved drawing, inspected finished part and named materials from the released schedule.

Protect service parts, prepare the verified substrates, place supported repairs and sealing layers in the approved order, and assemble the original Kohler components.

Check: Incoming dimensions, flatness, thickness, edges and finish accepted; sealing path continuous; service parts remain removable; drain unobstructed.

Stop: The current seated-cover trial has solid intersections and is rejected. No selected mortar, backing, sealant, finish or cure schedule exists. Do not install from this draft.

06

Cure, test and preserve access

Who: Installer; homeowner observes results
Before: Selected product-specific cure and water-exposure requirements met.
Tools: Approved test procedure, dry indicators and accessible inspection points.

First check supply/valve behavior with surfaces kept dry; then test controlled water exposure around the assembled trim while observing available access.

Check: Record product cure conditions, test duration/conditions, observed locations and results. Retain a future removal procedure.

Stop: Stop if moisture appears. Investigate supply leakage separately from surface-water entry. A local pass does not certify the concealed shower system.
