CLEARANCE BUDGET
D_hole_nominal >= D_stud_max + 2*(e_location + e_axis + e_install + e_cut_position + c_free) + delta_diameter_undersize
All e terms and c_free are worst-case radial allowances at the actual plate plane. delta_diameter_undersize is a diametral fabrication allowance. Obtain the vendor size/position convention so the same error is not counted twice. The formula is a sufficient conservative clearance budget for a round pass-through hole; it does not check bearing strength or seal land.
An assumed 0.5 mm radial location allowance alone consumes 1.0 mm of diametral clearance, before fabrication and free-assembly clearance. This is an illustration, not a measured allowance for this installation.
For any future required custom mounting holes, compare round clearance versus one locating hole and a slot along the stud-pair line only if bearing, restraint, drainage and seal land remain suitable. No change to factory holes is selected.
First reconcile the 3D endpoint geometry; then bound location at the real plate plane. Assembly clearance, measurement/registration uncertainty, machining tolerance, and seal-support requirements are separate quantities. Do not use depth corrections or fit residuals as manufacturing tolerances.
