# Physical checks for FT01

No full physical fit, received-part inspection or installed water test has been recorded. FT01 authorizes a quote and sacrificial fit trial; it is not a permanent-installation release. Earlier R001/F005/CB01/AP01 records remain historical evidence.

Use the [four-page fillable expert visit record](../../deliverables/current/fabrication/fit-trial/expert_visit_record_FT01.pdf) with the [current project guide](../../deliverables/current/fabrication/fit-trial/project_handoff_FT01.pdf). Save a new copy named with the actual date, observer and part revision. It can also be printed and completed by hand. Keep entries concise, attach longer notes and photos, and inspect the saved/printed copy for any hidden scrolling text. Repeat the record ID on all pages.

The four pages cover the received parts, factory attachment, the complete added stack, and the chosen repair/cure/testing. Every result starts blank or NOT RECORDED. PASS is the observer's stated result for that one check, not fabrication approval or proof of waterproofing. Mark hidden or untested interfaces UNRESOLVED and explain N/A. Do not enter a target dimension as a measured observation.

The equivalent [blank JSON](../../deliverables/current/fabrication/fit-trial/field_record_blank.json) and [field definitions](../../deliverables/current/fabrication/fit-trial/field_record_schema.json) retain the same names, page numbers and P01-P20 check IDs. The older `record_template.json` remains available for free-form check records. Completed forms must be reviewed against the current design and dated source evidence; no form import automatically changes canon or accepts a design. Do not edit or replace the blank templates with actual results.

For a paper trial, print the **two FT01 A3 landscape sheets** at **100% / actual size**, with fit-to-page disabled. Measure both 100 mm calibration bars on each page before cutting. Keep the main capsule upright and center each backing on its actual factory cover. The dashed 120 mm diverter circle is a fallback reference on the second sheet. The four-page templates from earlier studies are not the current recommendation. Paper verifies planar layout only, not support, stiffness, sealing or depth.

The expert first establishes factory handle/adapter/hub fit, then trials the original gasket, inner plate, cover and hardware with the actual added thickness and effective support/bedding height. A factory-only mechanical trial cannot establish a seal over the damaged opening. Record adapter and screw identities, seating, engagement, operation and actual before/after rod projections. A rod or stem visible through an opening is not proof of a properly engaged handle.

The owner confirms trim-rod contact with wood and prefers conditional clearance in the wood if the other valve/handle depths work. The expert determines safe access and the needed remedy/dimensions. No additional travertine cutting, drilling, grinding or enlargement is authorized. Do not force seating, discard factory parts or tighten hardware to overcome an unresolved interference. Prototype plastic is a fit trial, not an approved permanent wet-area material.

Trial both plates together. Record registration, centering/retention, coverage of every gouge including CUT-03, sound support, rocking/deflection, the entire actual gasket/contact path, cover seating, stud/nut/adapter/handle engagement, bottom drainage, full handle travel, neighbor clearance, tool/removal access and cartridge access. Preserve photos and raw readings with their method, date and observer. A changed support, aperture, material, thickness, adapter or seal detail invalidates the affected earlier fit conclusions.

Inspect a received part against its actual confirmed order: revision, material/finish, outside and inside profiles, thickness range, free flatness, edges and damage. Requested shop targets are not evidence that a vendor accepted or achieved them. Record deviations and recheck actual fit before considering permanent installation.

Record the expert's selected support/seal detail, substrate compatibility, product/primer identities and current technical instructions. Record cure conditions and the permitted water-exposure time from those instructions; no generic cure time is assumed. Log supply/valve leakage separately from shower spray ingress. Use accessible observation points and stop on new moisture. A successful local test does not certify the entire concealed shower system.
