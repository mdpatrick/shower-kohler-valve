# Physical checks — none performed at R001

Record each result against the actual model revision, date, observer, method, raw readings and photo reference. `record_template.json` is a blank form; it contains no pass results. A failing or changed condition invalidates dependent fit conclusions. Do not put an assumed target into the observation field.

For paper trials, print all four profile-template pages at **100% / actual size**, with fit-to-page disabled. Measure both 100 mm calibration bars on every page before cutting/taping. Align tile coordinates and overlap marks. Do not use a failed print for dimensions. Keep UP at the top; the aperture is AP-01 and the center datum is C. Paper verifies planar layout only. It is not a support, stiffness, sealing or depth test.

A later thickness trial must use a measured trial part or spacers for the proposed total added stack, original gasket/inner plate/outer trim and real hardware. Do not force seating, compress finishes, discard factory parts or alter studs. Prototype materials are not approved wet-area materials. The installer must establish safe access and whether pressure-containing components need isolation.

Dry-fit checks: actual valve registration; repeatable centering/retention; CUT-03 and every other defect; sound support beyond damage; plate rocking/deflection; entire gasket contact; outer-trim seating; stud/nut/adapter and handle engagement; drain at 6 o'clock and unblocked; handle travel; lower diverter clearance; cartridge access; removal sequence and tool clearance. Record which surfaces touched and where gaps remain.

Incoming inspection, after an approved part is manufactured: compare revision/material/finish to released order; measure overall profile, aperture and feature coordinates, thickness, flatness and edge treatment; inspect burrs, scratches, distortion and finish; reject unexplained changes. Repeat physical fit before permanent installation.

Installed testing is a separate record after the selected products' verified cure/water-exposure conditions. Record plumbing/supply checks separately from controlled surface-water entry checks. Maintain accessible observation points and stop on new moisture. A local successful test does not certify the whole concealed shower system.
